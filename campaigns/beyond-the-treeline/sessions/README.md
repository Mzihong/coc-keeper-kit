# sessions/

Per-session prep and recaps. Copy `templates/session-prep.md` to `<n>-<slug>.md` —
`1-the-drowned-chapel.md`, `2-what-the-ledger-said.md`, … Fill the top before you play
(recap, likely scenes, things to grab); `core/12-canon-update.md` fills the bottom after
(what happened, threads, SAN/injuries/items, prep for next time).

Session numbers are **global and continuous** — they don't reset when a new arc opens in this
campaign (`campaigns/README.md` → "Multi-arc & branching campaigns"), and `canon-log.md`
always cites the global number.

This is your continuity log — the running memory of the campaign.
