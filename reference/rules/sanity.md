# 7e Sanity — cheat-sheet

> The heart of the game. Sanity loss should cost something the player *feels*, not just a
> number. See the *Keeper Rulebook* for full rules.

## The numbers
- Starting **SAN = POW**. Max SAN = **99 − Cthulhu Mythos %**.
- A Sanity loss is written **X/Y** — lose **X** on a successful SAN roll, **Y** on a failure
  (e.g. `0/1D6`, `1/1D8`, `1D4/1D10`). Roll SAN (d100 ≤ current SAN) to resist.

## Typical loss values (guidance for `create-monster` / `description`)
| Trigger | Loss |
|---------|------|
| Surprised by a corpse; awful but mundane sight | 0/1D2 – 0/1D4 |
| A mangled body; witnessing a violent death | 0/1D4 – 1/1D6 |
| Seeing a minor Mythos entity / undead | 0/1D6 – 1/1D8 |
| A major/unnatural entity | 1/1D10 – 1D6/2D10 |
| A Great Old One / god | 1D10/1D100 |

Keep it proportionate. Reserve big losses for genuine Mythos revelation, not gore.

## Bout of madness
Losing **≥ 5 SAN from a single roll** → the investigator makes an **INT roll**; if they
succeed they grasp the horror and suffer a **bout of madness** (real-time ~1D10 rounds, or
summary ~1D10 hours). Roll or choose an effect (see `reference/tables/madness.md`).

## Temporary vs indefinite insanity
- **Temporary insanity:** triggered by a bout of madness; lasts until the scene resolves.
- **Indefinite insanity:** losing **≥ 20% of current SAN within one day** → weeks/months of
  a deeper disorder; the investigator functions but is unreliable under stress.

## Recovery
SAN returns slowly — resolving a scenario's threat, therapy, downtime, or a successful
Cthulhu Mythos-free rationalisation. Never a quick heal; dread should linger.

## Keeper habits
- Announce the SAN cost only *after* the sight is described — lead with the image.
- Track each investigator's phobias/manias; call them back for personalised horror.
- The first Mythos exposure is a milestone — make it land.
