# 7e Combat — cheat-sheet

> CoC combat is fast and lethal — investigators are fragile. Use it to raise stakes, not as
> the default answer. See the *Keeper Rulebook* for full rules.

## Order & actions
- Act in descending **DEX** order (firearms readied at the start of combat may go first).
- On your turn: one attack (or move + attack, manoeuvre, cast, etc.).

## Attacks & defence
- **Attacker** rolls the relevant fighting/firearms skill.
- **Melee defender** chooses to **Dodge** (Dodge skill; success avoids) or **Fight Back**
  (their own Fighting skill; if they win, they deal the damage instead).
- Higher **success level** wins the exchange; ties favour the defender / higher skill.
- A **firearms** target normally can't dodge bullets (only dive for cover) — ranged combat
  is deadlier and one-sided.

## Damage, Build & Damage Bonus
Derived from **STR + SIZ**:
| STR+SIZ | Damage Bonus | Build |
|---------|--------------|-------|
| 2–64    | −2   | −2 |
| 65–84   | −1   | −1 |
| 85–124  | none | 0  |
| 125–164 | +1D4 | +1 |
| 165–204 | +1D6 | +2 |
| 205–284 | +2D6 | +3 |
| 285–364 | +3D6 | +4 |

**Build** governs grapples/manoeuvres: you generally can't out-manoeuvre a target whose
Build is 2+ higher than yours without help.

## Hit points & wounds
- **HP = (CON + SIZ) ÷ 10** (round down).
- A **Major Wound** = single hit ≥ ½ max HP → roll CON or fall prone/unconscious; risk of
  dying without First Aid. **0 HP** = dying.
- **First Aid** (once per wound) restores 1 HP and stabilises; **Medicine** heals more over time.

## Manoeuvres
Disarm, shove, pin, grab — an opposed roll influenced by Build. Good for non-lethal or
cinematic beats without dealing damage.

## Keeper habits
- Telegraph lethality — a fair warning before the first fight teaches caution.
- Most monsters should be **outrun, tricked, warded, or endured**, not defeated in a fair
  fight. Let combat be the *failure* branch, not the goal.
- Track ammo and light; scarcity is scarier than tough enemies.
