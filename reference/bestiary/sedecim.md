# Sedecim (塞德西姆, "the Five")

*Two metres of hairless, grey-yellow skin, a skull twice the size it should be, and where a
nose and mouth belong, eight thin tendrils moving in a rhythm that isn't breathing.*

- **Type:** mythos servitor (formerly human)
- **Threat:** deadly
- **Sanity to see:** 0/1D6

## Reveal (say this out loud, before the SAN roll)

It doesn't approach so much as arrive — tall, wrong-proportioned, moving with a fluidity human
joints don't have. The tendrils where its mouth should be part slightly, and you understand,
without any sound being made, that it has already decided whether you're worth talking to.

### Combat

- **Attacks per round:** 1 (melee, grapple, or psychic blast)
- **Grapple** (maneuver) — Fighting 50%. A hit drags the target toward its head, where mouth
  tendrils bore in for automatic 1D6 damage per round until an opposed STR roll breaks free
  or the Sedecim drops below half HP (at which point it releases on its own).
- **Psychic blast** — automatic success, range 15m, single target. A failed POW roll stuns
  the target for 1D6 rounds and costs 1D6 SAN; the stunned target can still act, but every
  roll (combat included) takes a penalty die.
- **Pain Inducer** (device, not innate) — Firearms (Pain Inducer) 40%. On a hit, a difficult
  POW roll failure means agonising pain, collapse, and 1D6 rounds blind from bleeding eyes,
  fully stunned; success reduces this to 1D3 rounds with a penalty die instead of full
  incapacitation. Witnesses to its use roll Sanity (1D6 loss on failure). See
  `reference/mythos/artifacts-zh.md`.
- **Dodge:** 50%
- **Armour:** 1 point of tough, leathery skin.

### Special abilities

- **Amphibious+.** Breathes underwater, in vacuum, and on land.
- **Telepathy.** Communicates with humans within 15m; range between Sedecim is unknown. A
  target can attempt a POW roll to resist having their thoughts read.
- **Alien technology.** Can build devices that replicate known spell effects, powered by MP
  and POW per the spell's normal cost; ordinary people using such a device risk the same SAN
  cost the spell itself would carry.
- **Higher-dimensional access.** Can open paths to other dimensions and times (5 MP) and
  create semi-permanent pocket dimensions on Earth (10+ MP) — see
  `reference/mythos/artifacts-zh.md` → Non-Euclidean Architecture. Humans encountering or
  using such a space lose 1D4–1D8 SAN; travelling through one costs SAN and MP similar to a
  Find Gate working.
- **Knows 2D4 spells.**

## Behaviour & weakness

- **Wants / drive:** pursues its own research inside a private non-Euclidean chamber, barely
  interacting with the rest of its host organisation except to issue orders. Motives beyond
  "preparing for Cthulhu's return" are deliberately opaque, even to its own creators.
- **How it's usually beaten:** its combat stats are built to make direct confrontation a last
  resort. The fair outs are exploiting the fact that it rarely leaves its private chamber (an
  ambush there is a very different fight than intercepting it in the open), and that its
  telepathy can be resisted with a POW roll — cutting off its intelligence-gathering matters
  as much as fighting it.
- **Weakness / ward:** none innate — its danger is almost entirely from its abilities, not raw
  combat stats relative to its HP. A Keeper wanting a discoverable "out" should tie one to the
  specific campaign (a flaw in the Processor device that created it, a dependency on its host
  organisation's resources).

## Lore

> **KEEPER ONLY** — Sedecim are the end state of the Church of Perfect Science's "Final
> Processing" (see `reference/mythos/cults/church-of-perfect-science.md`) — five currently
> exist, the last transformation in 2004. They command the Church's Processed-4L tier but
> answer to no one within it; whether they serve Cthulhu's return or something else entirely
> is left open on purpose.
