# Cthulhu-Blessed (克苏鲁受祝者)

*Born to Deep One and human parents, but not a hybrid — this one came out already touched,
already changed, already the thing the family stopped talking about.*

- **Type:** independent-race (human-Deep One offspring, mutant strain)
- **Tier:** L2 creature
- **Threat:** deadly
- **Sanity to see:** 1/1D8 to 1D4/1D10
- **Serves:** Cthulhu (sacred to Deep Ones as his touched offspring)
- **Index summary:** Mutant Deep One-Cthulhu hybrid; a directed weapon, not a schemer

## Reveal (say this out loud, before the SAN roll)

Whatever it was born with — a tentacled face, a fused and finned lower body, half-grown bat
wings that never fully unfolded — it wasn't hidden as carefully as the family wanted, and now
it's coming toward you at a speed nothing that size should manage on land.

### Special abilities

- **Enhanced senses.** Climb 30%, Jump 50%, Listen 40%, Scent Prey 60%, Spot Hidden 40%,
  Stealth 60%, Swim 100%.
- **POW 70+ individuals have a chance of knowing 1D4 spells.**

## Behaviour & weakness

- **Wants / drive:** whatever the cult or family raising it directs — these individuals are
  usually developmentally disabled and kept hidden, brought out only for rituals or set loose
  to hunt a cult's enemies. Not a schemer; a weapon someone else points.
- **How it's usually beaten:** its combat stats are dangerous but it isn't tactically clever —
  a straightforward threat rather than a puzzle. The discoverable angle is almost always
  upstream: whoever is directing it, and why it's being deployed now.
- **Weakness / ward:** none innate beyond ordinary combat and evasion.
