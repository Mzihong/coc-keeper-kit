# Byakhee, the Star-Steeds (拜亚基)

*A wingbeat that belongs to no bird — leathery, too rhythmic, closing fast out of a clear
night sky.*

- **Type:** servitor-race
- **Tier:** L3 servitor
- **Threat:** moderate — malleus files Byakhee as 下级仆从种族 (lesser servitor race); per
  `reference/rules/monster-scale.md`'s 上级/下级 guidance, a lesser sub-tier entry takes the
  `trivial`/`moderate` rows of L3. Its HP (14–15) and attack skill (35%) sit almost exactly at
  the tier's `moderate` median, so that's the band, not `trivial`.
- **Sanity to see:** 1/1D6 (malleus's own figure for this entry)
- **Serves:** Hastur
- **Index summary:** Star-bat mount; drains STR on bite

## Reveal (say this out loud, before the SAN roll)

The sound arrives before the shape does — a wingbeat with a rhythm no bird or bat ever kept,
too regular, too patient. What crosses the stars overhead is furred and winged and jointed
wrong, crow-black and vulture-broad at once, and where a face should be there is only a
crumpled, eyeless suggestion of one. It carries something, or someone, without slowing.

### Combat

- **Attacks per round:** 1 — either two claw strikes on the same target, or a single bite.
- **Claw** — skill 35%, damage 1D6 + db (two strikes if both target the same foe).
- **Bite** — skill 35%, damage 1D6, then latches on and drains 1D6 STR per round until it dies,
  is killed, or the victim's STR reaches 0 (death). A latched byakhee keeps feeding rather than
  disengaging.
- **Dodge:** not stated in the source; ~26% (DEX×2) is a reasonable default if it matters.
- **Armour:** 2 points — fur and thick hide.

### Special abilities

- **Star-flight & void tolerance.** A byakhee crosses interstellar space unaided and can carry
  one rider — but the rider needs a spell or preparation (malleus names "space mead" as one
  option) to survive the cold and vacuum of the crossing. This is a plot lever, not a combat
  trait: a byakhee encountered on Earth is already within a breathable, warm envelope.
- **Spellcaster (conditional).** Any byakhee with POW over 14 knows 1D4 spells, almost always
  tied to Hastur or his kin (summonings, wards, other byakhee-related workings). Roll or decide
  per individual; most rank-and-file byakhee know none.
- **Skills:** Listen 50%, Spot Hidden 50% — they navigate and hunt well in open dark.

## Behaviour & weakness

- **Wants / drive:** it isn't hunting on its own account — a byakhee acts as mount, courier, or
  hired muscle for whoever holds the summoning bond (a cultist, a pact, occasionally Hastur or
  a fellow servitor directly). Loose, unbonded byakhee are rare and usually just passing through
  on the way to somewhere else.
- **How it's usually beaten:** malleus is explicit that its body, however alien, is made of
  ordinary matter — **conventional firearms hurt it just fine**, and with only 2 points of
  armour and 14–15 HP it doesn't take much sustained fire to bring one down. The horror is in
  the reveal, not in the stat card; a party that keeps its nerve and shoots survives the fight.
- **Weakness / ward:** the bite-drain is the real trap in prolonged combat (STR bleeds away
  1D6/round once latched), but it can be broken early — killing the byakhee, or driving it off
  before it finishes latching, stops the drain outright; a drained-but-alive victim recovers
  1D3 STR/day with rest and a transfusion. Investigating *why* a byakhee is present (who
  summoned it, what bond controls it) is the better long-game fair-out: cut the summoning and
  the mount stops coming.

## Lore

> **KEEPER ONLY** — Byakhee keep no nests on Earth; every one encountered here was summoned,
> whether as transport for a cultist crossing the void, a courier carrying a message or object
> for Hastur's cult, or muscle sent ahead of a more dangerous arrival (malleus notes they
> sometimes precede the Feaster from Afar). A byakhee sighting is rarely the threat itself — it's
> a sign that someone nearby has the means and will to call on Hastur's court, which is the
> actual thread worth pulling.
