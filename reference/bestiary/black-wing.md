# Black Wing (黑翼者)

*A man-shaped thing unfolds leathery wings from arms too long to be arms, and the mouth under
its eyeless face is an insect's mouth, wrong on a face that size.*

- **Type:** servitor-race
- **Tier:** L3 servitor
- **Threat:** deadly
- **Sanity to see:** 0/1D6
- **Serves:** Cthulhu (contested — see Lore; also theorised as Star-Spawn offshoot or independent)
- **Index summary:** Flying night hunter; snatches lone victims, can't be bound — only fed

## Reveal (say this out loud, before the SAN roll)

The sound comes first — a membrane snapping taut, once, twice, like a sail catching wind
that isn't there. Then the shape drops out of the dark above: a torso and limbs unmistakably
derived from something that once walked, wings stretched between arm and flank, and where a
face should be, only a knot of mandibles working around a mouth too full of teeth.

### Combat

- **Attacks per round:** 3 (two claws, one bite)
- **Claw** — Fighting 60%, 1D4+1D4 damage.
- **Bite** — Fighting 60%, 1D4+1 (tears free a chunk) or 1D4 (holds on — see *Carry Off*
  below).
- **Carry Off.** A held victim can attempt an opposed STR roll each round to break free.
  Otherwise the Black Wing lifts and either feeds mid-flight or drops the victim from height
  (1D6 damage per 3 metres fallen — roll 1D6, multiply by 3, for the height).
- **Dodge:** 40%
- **Armour:** 1 point of tough hide.
- **Echolocation.** Navigates and hunts by high-frequency calls; functions perfectly in total
  darkness and cannot be surprised by anything relying on stealth alone in the dark.

### Special abilities

- **10% chance of knowing 1D4 spells.**
- Rarely surfaces except at night; most sightings are a shape crossing the moon, not a clear
  look.

## Behaviour & weakness

- **Wants / drive:** to be fed. A Black Wing that answers a summons and finds no sacrifice
  waiting will either leave immediately or turn on whoever called it.
- **How it's usually beaten:** almost never in a fair fight — it flies, it hits three times a
  round, and it can carry a losing target away entirely. The discoverable outs are refusing
  it a sacrifice (it leaves or turns hostile, which is itself dangerous but at least
  predictable) or exploiting the fact that **it cannot be bound**, only placated — a cult that
  stops paying it stops controlling it.
- **Weakness / ward:** no binding spell exists for this creature (see
  `reference/mythos/spells/black-wing-call.md`) — the only leverage over one is the standing
  arrangement that keeps it fed. Cutting that arrangement is a fair, discoverable lever.

## Lore

> **KEEPER ONLY** — Origin is deliberately unsettled canon, not a single answer: some scholars
> read Black Wings as a variant of nightgaunt loyal to Cthulhu rather than Nodens; others as
> an offshoot of the Star-Spawn; others as a species that has always existed alongside Cthulhu,
> hunting for a way into the Dreamlands to spread his will there. Pick whichever serves the
> campaign, or leave it as an open question the investigators can't resolve.
>
> A historical sighting of a "black-winged demon" is already on record — see the 1645 Norfolk
> witch-coven entry in `reference/mythos/cthulhu-cult-history-zh.md`'s "黑翼者线" — usable as
> the creature's earliest attested appearance, or as a completely unrelated coincidence.
> The Louisiana Swamp Cult (`reference/mythos/cults/louisiana-swamp-cult.md`) keeps a standing
> arrangement with a roost, fed on a regular sacrifice schedule.
