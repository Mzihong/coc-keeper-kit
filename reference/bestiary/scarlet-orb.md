# Scarlet Orb (猩红者)

*A sphere of red-white light, no bigger than a fist, drifts at head height — and the warmth
coming off it feels like being forgiven for something you never did.*

- **Type:** servitor-race (extradimensional energy being)
- **Tier:** L3 servitor
- **Threat:** moderate (deadly in aggregate — see Radiation)
- **Sanity to see:** 0 (indirect — see Euphoric Vision)
- **Serves:** Cthulhu (theorised, unconfirmed — see Lore)
- **Index summary:** Drifting light; drains MP/SAN via euphoria and cumulative radiation

## Reveal (say this out loud, before the SAN roll)

There's no SAN roll for the sphere itself — that's the trap. It's beautiful: a floating coal
of red-white light the size of a fist, radiating a warmth that reaches the skin before the eye
finishes registering the colour. People smile before they know why.

### Combat

- **Attacks per round:** 1 (Burn), and only if provoked — a Scarlet Orb left alone rarely
  attacks first.
- **Burn** — 100% (automatic), 1+ damage to everyone within 1.5m. Costs 1 MP and deals 1
  damage per Orb present, +1 for every additional MP spent (two Orbs each spending 5 MP deal
  10 damage to everyone nearby). Heat-resistant clothing or being soaked in water can reduce
  or negate this at the Keeper's discretion.
- **Dodge:** —
- **Armour:** immune to mundane attacks; magic weapons, spells, and (at the Keeper's ruling)
  a sustained stream of water can affect or banish it. Summoned Orbs must spend 1 MP per
  minute to remain manifest and leave once they run out.

### Special abilities

- **Euphoric Vision.** Anyone nearby may experience visions of the past, future, or places
  beyond comprehension — an overwhelming sense of peace lasting 1D6+4 minutes, during which
  they're blind and deaf to the real world (taking damage snaps them out instantly). A
  difficult POW roll resists it; anyone already "opened" to the Mythos accepts automatically.
  The vision grants 1 Cthulhu Mythos point and, on a successful Sanity roll, +1 SAN (up to
  normal starting maximum) — failure costs 1D4 SAN. Unknown to the subject, the Orb drains
  1D6 MP from them during the experience (from HP if they have none left). Afterward, a POW
  roll that fumbles (rolls 100) causes a permanent change — Keeper's choice or roll 1D6:
  permanent loss of 1D10 or 1D4 POW, hair turning white, eye colour changing, a minor
  Keeper-chosen mutation, or a third eye growing on the forehead (sighted, blind, or seeing
  the unseen, Keeper's call).
- **Radiation.** Anyone within 1.5m for a cumulative 8+ minutes rolls CON or suffers headache,
  nausea, and weakness (penalty die on physical actions) for 2–6 hours; a fumble costs 1D4 HP
  and a penalty die on mental skills for 2D6 hours. Three failed CON rolls across repeated
  exposure starts radiation sickness — 2 HP/day until death, curable only by magic. A critical
  success on the CON roll grants permanent immunity.

## Behaviour & weakness

- **Wants / drive:** unclear even to Mythos scholars — theorised as either fragments of
  Cthulhu's dream given loose form, or an unrelated species descended from Fire Vampires.
  It does not seem to want anything from humans specifically.
- **How it's usually beaten:** rarely fought — its danger is cumulative exposure, not combat.
  The fair outs are **distance** (the radiation and vision effects both require proximity)
  and **water**, which drives it out of the material plane.
- **Weakness / ward:** cannot be bound by any known spell (see
  `reference/mythos/spells/scarlet-orb-call.md`) — only summoned and endured until its MP
  runs out.

## Lore
