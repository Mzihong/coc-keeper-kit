# Deep One Hybrid (混种深潜者)

*Born looking entirely human, and for twenty-odd years, entirely human is what they are.*

- **Type:** independent race (human-Deep One offspring)
- **Threat:** moderate
- **Sanity to see:** 0/1D4 (higher for a visibly advanced individual — 1/1D6+)

## Reveal (say this out loud, before the SAN roll)

Nothing, at first — that's the point. The wrongness arrives on a schedule: a slight widening
of the eyes, a stiffness at the neck that could be a old injury, a tendency to stand too still
near open water. By the time it's unmistakable, the transformation is already years along.

### Combat

- **Attacks per round:** 1 (unarmed or weapon — fights exactly like a human, firearms
  included)
- **Fighting** 45%, 1D3+db or by weapon.
- **Dodge:** 30%
- **Armour:** none.

### Special abilities

- **Double breath-hold.** Cannot breathe underwater until fully transformed, but can hold
  their breath twice as long as a baseline human from childhood on.
- **10% chance (older, self-accepting hybrids) of knowing 1D3 spells.**

## Behaviour & weakness

- **Wants / drive:** whatever they wanted before the changes started — a hybrid is a person
  first. Later-stage individuals increasingly withdraw, embarrassed or afraid of their own
  appearance, or are hidden by family/community rather than acting against anyone.
- **How it's usually beaten:** rarely a combat threat on its own — it fights like an ordinary
  person because, mechanically, it mostly still is one. The interesting conflict is almost
  always social or investigative, not violent.
- **Weakness / ward:** none needed — this is a transformation stage, not a monster to defeat.
  The "threat" framing above is for the rare hybrid pressed into violence by a cult, not the
  default case.

## Lore

> **KEEPER ONLY** — The default outcome of the Esoteric Order of Dagon's third Oath of Dagon
> (see `reference/mythos/cults/esoteric-order-of-dagon.md`), though the pairing doesn't have
> to be that cult specifically — any coastal community with a Deep One arrangement produces
> the same pattern. Full transformation to a true Deep One typically completes sometime
> between the individual's thirties and seventies; a minority never fully transform and stay
> in this intermediate state indefinitely.
