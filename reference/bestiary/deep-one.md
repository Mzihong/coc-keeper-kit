# Deep One, Gilled Humanoid (深潜者)

*What surfaces from the reef isn't quite a fish and isn't quite a man, and it has been hunting longer than either.*

- **Type:** servitor-race
- **Tier:** L3 servitor
- **Threat:** moderate — malleus files the Deep One as 下级仆从种族 (lesser servitor race); per
  `reference/rules/monster-scale.md`'s 上级/下级 guidance, a lesser sub-tier takes the
  trivial/moderate rows of L3, not deadly/mythic. Its HP (13–14) and Sanity loss (0/1D6) sit at
  the top of the moderate band; attack skill (25%) undercuts moderate's usual floor slightly —
  but a Deep One almost never fights alone, and the net-entangle-then-swarm pattern plays as
  moderate at the table, not trivial. Numbers transcribed directly from
  `reference/sourcebooks/malleus-monstrorum-zh.md`'s Deep One entry.
- **Sanity to see:** 0/1D6 (malleus's own figure for first sighting)
- **Serves:** Cthulhu, Dagon & Hydra
- **Index summary:** Deep One; claw, spear, coastal ambush

## Reveal (say this out loud, before the SAN roll)

The smell reaches the beach before it does — brine gone sour, something that should have
finished rotting and hasn't. Grey-green skin catches what little light there is in an
unbroken, faintly slick sheen, broken only by a ridge of hard scale running down the spine; a
row of gill-slits along the throat flutter open and shut in a rhythm no human chest keeps.
The eyes never blink. It doesn't so much walk as lurch — upright for a stride, down on all
fours for the next — closing the distance faster than that gait has any right to.

### Combat

- **Attacks per round:** 1 (claw, hunting spear, or fishing net — varies by individual and
  what's on hand; Deep Ones favour ambush and rarely fight one at a time)
- **Claw** — skill 25%, damage 1D6 + db.
- **Hunting spear** — skill 25%, damage 1D6 + db.
- **Fishing net** — skill 15%, no direct damage; entangles the target instead, setting up a
  follow-up attack (from this Deep One or another) against a target that can't easily flee.
- **Dodge:** 25%
- **Armour:** 1 point (scaled skin) — no immunity clause; ordinary weapons harm it normally at
  this tier.

### Special abilities

None from `reference/tables/monster-traits.md` — a baseline Deep One is dangerous through
numbers, ambush, and its home terrain, not a bolt-on mechanic. (A rare individual variant, the
*Shoggoth-Thsya* — a high priest able to bind and direct a shoggoth — exists in malleus but is
its own, much rarer statline; treat it as a unique NPC if it comes up, not the default Deep
One.) Roughly a quarter of individuals (POW > 70 on the scale above) know 1D4 Cthulhu Mythos
spells tied to Cthulhu, Dagon, Hydra, the Star-Spawn of Cthulhu, or other deep servitors —
Keeper's choice which.

## Behaviour & weakness

- **Wants / drive:** to defend its city and kin, to enforce whatever bargain the local
  Deep One community holds with a nearby human settlement, and — outside of conflict — to
  breed, worship Cthulhu, and otherwise be left alone at the bottom of the sea. Deep Ones are
  effectively ageless unless killed outright; an individual encountered today may be centuries
  old.
- **How it's usually beaten:** as a fighting force it's a tough but mortal humanoid — HP 13,
  one point of armour, nothing that shrugs off gunfire. The fair out is rarely "win the fight
  cleaner": Deep Ones hunt in groups, favour water (full mobility, ambush, an easy retreat) and
  are considerably easier to deal with on dry land, away from the surf, where they lose their
  home-terrain advantage and their numbers. Staying out of the water and off the beach at night
  avoids the encounter more often than fighting it.
- **Weakness / ward:** no innate magical vulnerability — the real ward is diplomatic, not
  mechanical. Deep Ones turn violent when a pact is broken (fishing rights ignored, a
  sacrifice withheld, a settlement's secrets threatened with exposure); honouring or
  renegotiating that arrangement ends hostilities without a fight, and finding out the pact's
  terms is itself the investigation.
