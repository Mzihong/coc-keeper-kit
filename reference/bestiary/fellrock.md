# Fellrock (费尔罗克)

*An elephant-sized, armoured, six-legged thing hauls itself half out of a collapsed tunnel
mouth, an eyeless trunk swinging toward the sound of your breathing.*

- **Type:** independent race
- **Threat:** deadly
- **Sanity to see:** 1D3/1D10

## Reveal (say this out loud, before the SAN roll)

The tunnel wall doesn't collapse so much as *open* — plates of reptilian hide the colour of
wet stone shifting apart around a body the size of a delivery truck, six legs (the front pair
massive, each pair behind smaller) finding purchase on rubble. The trunk that swings toward
you ends not in a nostril but in a fringed, working mouth.

### Combat

- **Attacks per round:** 1–2 (claws / trunk grapple) or 1 (stomp)
- While burrowed, only the trunk grapple is available. Half-surfaced or fully exposed, it can
  attack twice a round with claws, trunk, or one of each.
- **Trunk grapple** (maneuver) — Fighting 40%. A hit means the target is seized; the trunk
  restricts all movement next round. By the end of round two the target can attempt an
  opposed STR or DEX roll against the trunk's effective STR 100 to break free, or fight back.
  By round three, an un-freed target is drawn into the mouth and crushed for 4D6 damage per
  round. A merciful Keeper can rule that serious damage to the creature makes it drop
  whatever it's holding.
- **Stomp** (maneuver) — Fighting 40%, 4D6+2, hits up to 1D3 targets within a couple of metres
  of each other.
- **Dodge:** 20%
- **Armour:** 6 points of scaled hide.

### Special abilities

- **Telepathy.** Fellrock within 15m of each other share complex thoughts and memories as a
  tight-knit group. Can attempt to force a link with an unwilling human or other Mythos being
  of INT 15+ via opposed POW roll; the target can sever it by winning. A forced link that
  fails still lets the Fellrock spend 5 MP to skim surface thoughts.
- **Torpor.** Dormant roughly 80% of the year; slow metabolism means one large meal (a
  human-sized creature) can sustain it for months of digestion. Groups of four to eight,
  centuries-long lifespans, and rare — deaths are rare enough that reproduction is tied to
  losing a group member.
- **Oil of Silence** (see `reference/mythos/artifacts-zh.md`) repels them by smell; someone
  coated head to toe is safe from an unprovoked Fellrock, though provoking one still draws an
  attack (at a penalty die).

## Behaviour & weakness

- **Wants / drive:** food, then centuries of digesting it in peace. Not malicious — territorial
  and hungry, closer to a force of nature than a schemer.
- **How it's usually beaten:** rarely fought head-on — six-hundred-plus HP-equivalent stats
  make that a last resort. The fair outs are **avoidance** (it spends most of its life
  dormant and stays underground), **Oil of Silence** as a discoverable ward, and **feeding
  it something else** before it decides the investigators are dinner.
- **Weakness / ward:** the telepathic link is a two-way door — a psychic or Mythos-aware
  investigator forcing their way into a Fellrock's mind (reversing the usual direction) can
  in principle learn what it knows, including where the rest of its group sleeps.

## Lore

> **KEEPER ONLY** — Many Fellrock groups serve Cthulhu specifically; some scholars suspect the
> species is a byproduct or offshoot of the Star-Spawn, unconfirmed. `Society of the Divine
> Children` (`reference/mythos/cults/society-of-the-divine-children.md`) keeps a standing
> arrangement with a group near San Bernardino, trading two to three human sacrifices a year
> for the group's continued goodwill and silence — the cult's founder was contacted through
> dreams, not sought them out. `reference/mythos/spells/fellrock-call-bind.md` has the
> summon/bind ritual.
