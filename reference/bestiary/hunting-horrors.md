# Hunting Horrors (恐怖猎手)

*A forty-foot ribbon of black, rippling wing-flesh drops out of the dark, and the shape
underneath it can't seem to decide what it is.*

- **Type:** servitor-race
- **Tier:** L3 servitor
- **Threat:** deadly — malleus files Hunting Horrors as **上级仆从种族** (greater servitor
  race), and most of the stat block sits at the top of L3's `deadly` band or edges into
  `mythic` (HP 26–27, armour 9 plus firearm immunity, tail attack at 90%) — but the source's
  own Sanity loss (0/1D10) is well below the typical `mythic` range (1D3/1D20+), so `deadly`
  is the more honest read of the whole block, not just its scariest number.
- **Sanity to see:** 0/1D10
- **Serves:** Nyarlathotep (used as his hunting hounds; will also answer an unrelated summons
  if paid in blood and living sacrifice)
- **Index summary:** Flying hound; shuns light, guns useless

## Reveal (say this out loud, before the SAN roll)

The sound arrives before the shape does — a leathery snap, too big for any bird, cutting the
wind above the treeline. Then the silhouette resolves: forty feet of segmented black
serpent-body under one vast membranous wing, the outline rippling as if it can't settle on a
final shape. Its head lolls at an angle no spine should allow, and when it opens its mouth the
noise that comes out is a droning, bass syllable that belongs to no throat.

### Combat

- **Attacks per round:** 2 (bite and tail strike, used simultaneously)
- **Bite** — skill 65% (85% against a target already held by its tail), damage 1D6.
- **Tail** — skill 90%, entangles the target instead of dealing direct damage; the entangled
  victim can be dragged off in flight or held for follow-up bites. A held victim can attempt an
  opposed STR roll each round to break free, and cannot attack while entangled (spellcasting
  may still be possible).
- **Dodge:** not stated — treat as unable to dodge in the conventional sense; its evasion is
  its speed and its refusal to be where the light is.
- **Armour:** 9 points against everything else; conventional firearms cannot penetrate it at
  all, full stop, regardless of the armour math.

### Special abilities (traits)

- **Grapple & drag** (`monster-traits.md`) — a hit with the tail entangles the target, who
  automatically suffers grapple effects next round unless they win an opposed STR roll to break
  free. *Counter-play:* the escape roll is ordinary STR, not Extreme — a fit investigator has a
  real chance to shrug it off before being carried away. **Load 1.**
- **Flight** (`monster-traits.md`) — ignores most ground obstacles at Fly 11, hunting from
  above and dropping suddenly. *Counter-play:* confined interior spaces (tunnels, stairwells,
  a low-ceilinged room) strip it of the room to manoeuvre; it also will not willingly enter a
  brightly lit space, which doubles as a way to ground it (see below). **Load 1.**
- **Trait load total:** 2 / 2 (L3 servitor ceiling).

## Behaviour & weakness

- **Wants / drive:** it hunts because it is sent to hunt — a dog on a leash for whichever
  Mythos power called it, most often Nyarlathotep. Left to itself it still needs to eat; an
  unpaid summons demands blood and a living creature to satisfy it, or it turns on whoever
  summoned it.
- **How it's usually beaten:** never in a straight fight if it can be avoided — its armour
  stops bullets outright and its tail can simply carry a victim away. The discoverable fair out
  is **light**: Hunting Horrors avoid sunlight on principle, and anything close to true daylight
  intensity (a magnesium flare, a searchlight, direct sun) drives it off; extreme light sources
  (the text specifically notes something on the order of a nuclear flash) can wither and
  incinerate it outright. A party that works out "it hunts by night and won't cross a lit
  street" has found its actual weakness without ever needing a bigger gun.
- **Weakness / ward:** sustained bright light — findable by a survivor's account, a folklore
  source, or simple pattern-matching across several sightings, all of which happened after dark
  or indoors.

## Lore

> **KEEPER ONLY** — Hunting Horrors are the standard "elite guard" answer whenever a scene
> calls for something that answers to Nyarlathotep specifically (see
> `reference/mythos/great-old-ones/nyarlathotep.md`). They also turn up loose in Dreamlands
> scenes, sent to carry out an errand rather than guard anything. A pair of them is common
> around one of Nyarlathotep's human-passing masks (see
> [Black Pharaoh](black-pharaoh.md)) — invisible until their master orders them to show
> themselves, which is itself a usable tell that something is about to escalate.
