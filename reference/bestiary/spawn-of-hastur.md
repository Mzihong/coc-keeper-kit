# Spawn of Hastur (哈斯塔之眷族)

*Something breaches the lake's black surface without a splash, octopoid and huge, and does not
so much swim toward the shore as arrive there.*

- **Type:** servitor-race
- **Tier:** L3 servitor
- **Threat:** mythic — malleus files Spawn of Hastur as 上级仆从种族 (greater servitor race);
  per `reference/rules/monster-scale.md`'s 上级/下级 guidance, a greater sub-tier entry takes
  the `deadly`/`mythic` rows of L3. Its attack skill (75%) sits exactly at the greater
  sub-tier's median and its Sanity loss (1D6/1D20) sits at the top of the `mythic` band, so
  `mythic` fits better than `deadly`.
- **Sanity to see:** 1D6/1D20 (malleus's own figure for this entry)
- **Serves:** Hastur
- **Index summary:** Amphibious grappler; swallow = death

## Reveal (say this out loud, before the SAN roll)

The lake surface tents upward long before anything breaks it, a slow black dome of water with
no wind behind it. What rises is boneless and vast, an octopus's silhouette scaled up past
sense, its face — when it turns toward you — closer to a skull than to anything with a mouth
should be. It does not thrash. It reaches.

### Combat

- **Attacks per round:** 1 — a flowing-tentacle strike that either crushes or grapples.
- **Tentacle (crush)** — skill 75%, damage 1D6 + db.
- **Tentacle (grapple & swallow)** — skill 75%; a successful grapple hauls the target into its
  rubbery, sack-like body whole. This is an **automatic-kill grab** — malleus states the
  swallow is instant death for the victim, no further roll. Treat the grapple attempt itself as
  what a Keeper calls for a Dodge or STR-opposed escape against, not the swallow that follows it.
- **Dodge:** not stated in the source; ~20% (DEX×2) is a reasonable default if it matters.
- **Armour:** 10 points — thick, gelatinous hide.

### Special abilities

- **Amphibious, tied to Lake of Hali.** Spawn of Hastur are aquatic-to-amphibious and are
  recorded almost nowhere except the "foul, murky Lake of Hali" where Hastur himself manifests.
  Limited flight and short overland movement let one leave the water, but it is not built for
  sustained travel on land.
- **Bound to Aldebaran's rising.** Exactly like their father, Spawn of Hastur can only be
  summoned to Earth while Aldebaran (毕宿五) sits above the horizon. This is a hard scheduling
  constraint a Keeper can telegraph in advance — an almanac, an astronomer NPC, a cultist's
  ritual calendar all make it discoverable rather than arbitrary.
- **Spellcaster.** Every individual knows Summon/Contact Hastur and at least 2D6 further spells,
  generally drawn from the set tied to Hastur and his servitor races — treat a captured or
  bargaining-minded specimen as a genuine (if monstrous) source of Mythos knowledge, not just a
  combat encounter.
- **Skill:** Swim (underwater stealth) 35% — it closes distance in water largely unseen.

## Behaviour & weakness

- **Wants / drive:** these are Hastur's own get, not a hired servitor race — they linger near
  Hali because that is where they were spawned and where their father is most likely to appear.
  Left alone they guard the lake and its shoreline rather than ranging out to hunt; a cult
  wanting one active elsewhere has to bring the lake's conditions (or a piece of it) along.
- **How it's usually beaten:** with 10 points of armour, 25 HP, and an instant-kill swallow on
  a successful grapple, this is not a fair straight fight for an investigator-level party —
  treat combat as the failure branch. The grapple attempt is the one moment with a real escape
  window: a target who avoids or breaks the initial tentacle grab never reaches the swallow at
  all, so distance, cover, and not standing still near the shoreline are the practical defence.
- **Weakness / ward:** the Aldebaran-rising constraint above is the genuine strategic out — a
  party that identifies the astronomical window (or the ritual substitute a cult uses to fake
  one) can simply not be at the lake when a Spawn can be called, or can time an assault on the
  cult for the hours it cannot answer. That's discoverable through star charts, an astronomer,
  or a captured cultist's calendar, not a hidden secret.

## Lore

> **KEEPER ONLY** — Some fragmentary sources conflate Spawn of Hastur with Cthulhu's
> Star-Spawn, but malleus is explicit they are not the same race, however similar the silhouette
> reads at a distance. A cult that keeps a shrine at a Hali-like lake and times its rites to
> Aldebaran's rising is functionally raising these as a standing guard — a strong option for the
> "elite guard" a King-in-Yellow-tier campaign boss would actually post.
