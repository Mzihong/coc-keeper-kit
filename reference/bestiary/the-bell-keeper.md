# The Bell-Keeper

*A drowned lighthouse-keeper's watch that never ended — and never will, as long as the bell
above the rocks keeps ringing.*

- **Type:** servitor-race (undead)
- **Tier:** L3 servitor
- **Threat:** deadly
- **Sanity to see:** 1D4/1D10
- **Serves:** Independent — serves no god (bound to its lighthouse, not a cult or deity)
- **Index summary:** Undead lighthouse keeper; banished by matching his true bell pattern

## Reveal (say this out loud, before the SAN roll)

The dragging sound has been getting closer for a full minute before the fog finally thins
enough to show its source — a shape the height of a doorway, moving low across the rocks on
limbs that bend twice at each joint. Where its face should catch the lantern light, there is
only a crust the colour and texture of a barnacle-covered piling, and from somewhere inside
it, without any mouth you can find, comes the sound of a bell — one measure behind the real
bell on the tower, and getting closer to matching it.

### Combat

- **Attacks per round:** 1
- **Waterlogged Grip** — Fighting (Grapple) 55%, 1D6 + db, and the target is seized (see
  *Drowning Grip* below).
- **Dodge:** does not evade — it is slow and telegraphed; outrunning it on land is a real,
  discoverable option, not a bluff.
- **Armour:** 8 points against edged and impact weapons — its flesh has grown coral-hard.
  Firearms do half damage, rounded down. Fire ignores this armour entirely.

### Special abilities

- **Drowning Grip.** A grappled victim's lungs begin filling with seawater regardless of
  whether they are anywhere near water — CON roll each round or take 1D4 damage from
  suffocation, on top of any grip damage, until they break free (opposed STR) or the
  Bell-Keeper is destroyed or driven off.
- **Refuses to manifest.** Outside of a direct confrontation, it is heard and smelled, not
  seen — a dragging sound, the reek of low tide out of season, gulls going silent. Reserve a
  full physical sighting for the scene where the investigators corner it or it corners them.

## Behaviour & weakness

- **Wants / drive:** to keep the bell ringing on schedule, as it did in life, and to drag
  under anyone who lets it stop or interferes with the rhythm.
- **How it's usually beaten:** it cannot be reasoned with, but it can be **outrun** (see
  Dodge above), **warded** by keeping the bell ringing on its original cadence, or **banished**
  by someone who learns the drowned keeper's actual watch pattern and rings the bell in that
  broken rhythm instead — turning its own signal against it. Combat against it in full armour
  is close to unwinnable and should read that way at the table.
- **Weakness / ward:** the original watch log (kept by whoever replaced the drowned keeper, or
  found among the station's old paperwork) records the exact bell pattern he was keeping the
  night he died. Ringing that pattern is the discoverable "out" — a clue, not a stat.

## Lore

> **KEEPER ONLY** — Bound to whichever lighthouse or signal station it drowned defending; it
> does not travel far from that structure. If the campaign has a coastal cult or Deep One
> presence, the Bell-Keeper can be reframed as something the cult deliberately maintains (by
> keeping the bell ringing) to keep outsiders off a stretch of coastline it wants undisturbed
> — cross-link to `reference/mythos/` if that tie is used.
