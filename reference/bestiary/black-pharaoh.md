# Black Pharaoh (黑法老)

*He doesn't so much enter a room as the room arrives at him — every flame leaning toward his
silhouette at once, the air drawing close like a held breath.*

- **Type:** unique-entity
- **Tier:** L4 unique
- **Threat:** deadly — malleus's raw numbers (HP 15, SAN loss 0/1D2) read closer to L4's
  `trivial` band on their own, but the sceptre attack auto-hits for a flat 20 damage (no roll
  to land it), he knows any spell the Keeper allows, and he heals 1D6 HP for every Magic Point
  he spends — three things that make a straight fight far more dangerous than the HP total
  suggests. `deadly` reflects what actually happens at the table, not just the two smallest
  numbers on the sheet.
- **Sanity to see:** 0/1D2
- **Serves:** Is an avatar of Nyarlathotep — not a separate servant answering to him. Treat
  encountering the Black Pharaoh as encountering Nyarlathotep himself in one specific mask, not
  as meeting one of his subordinates (see `reference/mythos/great-old-ones/nyarlathotep.md`).
  If you're building an "elite guard" for a Nyarlathotep-themed scene, this entry *is* the boss
  wearing a different face, not the guard in front of the boss's door.
- **Index summary:** Avatar of Nyarlathotep; auto-hit blast

## Reveal (say this out loud, before the SAN roll)

He is tall, dark, and terribly handsome, wrapped in linen too clean for anything this old; his
smile has the calm of a man who has already seen how tonight ends. Somewhere close by, the air
keeps twisting on itself for no reason anyone can find. When he raises the twin sceptres,
nobody in the room can look away.

### Combat

- **Attacks per round:** 1
- **Sceptre blast** — automatic hit, no roll required, damage 20 flat (ignores armour on the
  target). He needs no specific action or exposed weapon to use it; the beam simply happens.
  Anyone struck by it is burned to a charred husk on the spot.
- **Dodge:** not applicable — he does not need to evade anything a mortal can bring to bear.
- **Armour:** none. Conventional weapons do full damage. In exchange, he can spend 1 Magic
  Point as a free action to heal 1D6 HP, up to his MP total (20) per encounter.
- **Spells:** knows any spell the Keeper judges appropriate to the scene.

### Special abilities (traits)

- **Trait load total:** 0 / 3 (L4 unique ceiling). No bolt-on traits from
  `reference/tables/monster-traits.md` were added — the native malleus abilities above
  (unavoidable attack, MP-funded regeneration, open-ended spell access) already carry this
  entry's full L4 weight, and stacking a numeric trait on top of a 15-HP body would understate
  how genuinely killable he is if you can out-damage the healing.
- **Hidden escort.** He is frequently attended by a pair of invisible Hunting Horrors (see
  [Hunting Horrors](hunting-horrors.md)) that only manifest on his direct order — an
  unexplained warping of the air nearby is the tell that they're present before they show
  themselves.

## Behaviour & weakness

- **Wants / drive:** to preside — over a cult's triumph, over an investigator's ruin, over a
  quiet warning to anyone who's gotten too close to old secrets. He rarely acts in a hurry;
  cruelty delivered as theatre interests him more than an efficient kill.
- **How it's usually beaten:** this is the rare Mythos entity where conventional weapons
  actually work at full damage — no armour to grind through — but he heals 1D6 HP per Magic
  Point spent, so a fight only goes the investigators' way if they can deal damage faster than
  he can spend MP to undo it, or find a way to stop him spending it at all (binding magic,
  interrupting a cast, denying him the round he needs). Against a party without any way to
  pressure his MP, fleeing or invoking a warding rite tied specifically to the Black Pharaoh
  Brotherhood's own countermagic is the safer path.
- **Weakness / ward:** no armour means brute damage output is a legitimate answer if it's fast
  enough; MP 20 caps how long his self-healing can hold out, which is discoverable by anyone
  who watches him heal and counts.

## Lore

> **KEEPER ONLY** — Worshipped by the Black Pharaoh Brotherhood, a cult centred on Cairo with a
> strong chapter in England; the mask also has looser ties to other Nyarlathotep-worshipping
> groups elsewhere. The malleus source notes an unresolved relationship between the Black
> Pharaoh and the historical Egyptian sorcerer-king Nephren-Ka — they may be the same figure, or
> Nephren-Ka may simply have borrowed the name to terrify his enemies; leave this open rather
> than resolving it, it's a usable thread on its own (see
> `reference/mythos/great-old-ones/nyarlathotep.md`'s "裁定空间" pattern of leaving
> mask/identity questions to the Keeper). If this mask's HP is reduced to 0,
> consider ruling it dismissed or banished back into Nyarlathotep rather than destroyed — an
> avatar with 0 HP tonight can still wear this same face again in a later session; that's a
> Keeper call, not a hard rule.
