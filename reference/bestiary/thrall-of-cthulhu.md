# Thrall of Cthulhu (克苏鲁之隶)

*What used to be a devoted cultist is now a swollen grey-blue mass, hairless, slit-eyed,
dragging itself forward on legs too short for the weight above them.*

- **Type:** mythos servitor (formerly human)
- **Threat:** deadly
- **Sanity to see:** 1/1D8

## Reveal (say this out loud, before the SAN roll)

The smell arrives before the shape does — something like marsh gas, something like rot that
hasn't finished. What used to be a person is now a bloated, gelatine-textured mass, unmoving
tentacles ringing a mouth of curved teeth, its remaining voice a clicking, wet stutter that no
human throat should be able to make.

## Stat block (CoC 7e)

Human characteristics adjusted by the transformation (see *Special abilities*). Standard and
mature (elder) profiles below.

| | STR | CON | SIZ | DEX | INT | POW |
|---|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| **Standard** | 75 | 75 | 85 | 30 | 65 | 55 |
| **Mature (50–100+ yrs)** | 120 | 120 | 130 | 30 | 65 | 80 |

- **HP** 16 (mature 25) · **Build** +1 (mature +3) · **Damage Bonus** +1D4 (mature +2D6) ·
  **MP** 11 (mature 16) · **Move** 6 / Swim 10

### Combat

- **Attacks per round:** 1 (claw, bite, sweep, crush, or squeeze — varies by individual, some
  can wield weapons, none can use firearms)
- **Fighting** 40%, 1D6+2+db or by weapon.
- **Dodge:** 15%
- **Armour:** none, but see *Undying* below.

### Special abilities

- **Transformation.** On conversion, STR/DEX/SIZ each +20 (note: source text also lists DEX
  -20 alongside the +20 — apply the net figure your campaign's Thrall should have; the
  standard-profile numbers above already reflect a coherent build). POW and INT are
  unchanged; EDU and APP no longer apply; SAN drops to 0 if not already there. Retains most
  human skills at first — social skills like Charm, Fast Talk, and Persuade linger but fade to
  uselessness over time. Every ten years afterward, STR/CON/SIZ each gain +1D10, plus
  whatever blessing Cthulhu chooses to grant (a spell, a POW increase, an extra attack).
- **Undying.** Regenerates 1D6 HP per round. Reduced to 0 HP by physical damage, it dissolves
  into a grey, foul-smelling gas and reforms in 1D8+1 rounds at full stats. Reduced to 0 HP by
  magic (or a combination of physical and magical harm) dies permanently. After several
  decades, it loses this regenerative ability entirely unless fully submerged in seawater.
- **40% chance of knowing 1D6+1 spells.**

## Behaviour & weakness

- **Wants / drive:** to serve Cthulhu, having lost essentially everything else. After 50–100
  years, mature Thralls retreat permanently into the ocean and join Deep Ones, Star-Spawn, and
  other deep servants — an old Thrall is rarely encountered on land at all.
- **How it's usually beaten:** the *Undying* trait makes ordinary combat a losing proposition
  — it just reforms. The fair out is **magical damage**, the only thing that ends it for good;
  a party without access to any is better off fleeing or trapping it (underwater submersion,
  for instance, restores its regeneration rather than hindering it, so that's not a valid
  trap).
- **Weakness / ward:** none beyond the magic-damage requirement above — a straightforward,
  discoverable combat fact rather than a hidden ritual.

## Lore

> **KEEPER ONLY** — Conversion can be selected or random, taking anywhere from years to
> minutes. A cult wanting a disposable, terrifying enforcer that can't permanently die without
> magical intervention has an obvious use for one; a cult member who *becomes* one is a
> character-arc hook, not just a monster stat block.
