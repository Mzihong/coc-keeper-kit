# Vermisyn Worm (弗米森蠕虫)

*A hookworm the length of a car surfaces in the sewer light — segmented, eyeless, its small
misshapen mouth working around something wet it's about to say to you.*

- **Type:** independent-race
- **Tier:** L2 creature
- **Threat:** deadly
- **Sanity to see:** 1D2/1D8
- **Serves:** Cthulhu (indirectly — serves his other servants more often than him directly; see Lore)
- **Index summary:** Sewer worm with 6m reach; shares memory, feeds on Mythos knowledge

## Reveal (say this out loud, before the SAN roll)

Fifteen to twenty feet of mottled, weeping flesh fills the tunnel, tapering at both ends,
studded with finger-length pseudopods that ripple independently of each other. It has no
eyes. It doesn't need them.

### Combat

- **Attacks per round:** 1 (slam, organ-lash, or crush)
- **Organ-lash** — Fighting 50%, range 6m. On a hit, target rolls CON+POW or is stunned by
  the combined physical/psychic shock. Next round the worm reels the organ back in, dragging
  the victim with it — an opposed STR or DEX roll against the worm's STR breaks free while
  still conscious; failing means being pulled into its stomach to dissolve, 1D2 damage per
  round until death or rescue (killing or badly wounding the worm frees anyone inside without
  further harm). One worm can hold up to two medium-sized victims this way, at half Move.
- **Crush** — Fighting 30%, 1D6+1D6, the worm rears and drops its body on the target, or coils
  to suffocate.
- **Slam** — Fighting 45%, 1D4+2 (a pseudopod strike; too small and clustered for real
  precision, sticky enough to grip and lift small objects but not wield weapons).
- **Dodge:** 20%
- **Armour:** none.

### Special abilities

- **Amphibious.** Breathes on land and underwater.
- **Communion.** Two or more worms can link by extending part of their viscera to another
  worm's — or to a human's, at the cost of a Sanity roll (1D10 loss on failure) — sharing
  memory and information directly.
- **Knowledge feast.** A worm that offers itself to be eaten by another Mythos servant of
  Cthulhu passes on strange knowledge: +10% Cthulhu Mythos, 1D8+4 SAN loss, to whoever
  consumes it. Investigators who eat Vermisyn flesh (deliberately or otherwise) get the same.
- **40% chance of knowing 1D4 spells.**

## Behaviour & weakness

- **Wants / drive:** to feed, and — unusually for a Mythos beast — to *know*. It actively
  seeks out communion with other servants of Cthulhu.
- **How it's usually beaten:** its reach (6m organ-lash) makes melee risky, but it's slow
  (Move 5 on land) and has no ranged options beyond that lash — distance and mobility are the
  fair out. A victim being digested can be pulled free without further harm if the worm takes
  serious damage.
- **Weakness / ward:** the organ-lash is its only long-range threat and it must reel a caught
  victim in over a full round — a rescuer with one clear round to act can intervene before
  digestion starts.
